# Yogurt-style pilot (public sample data)

A real plant-based yogurt-style pilot: 10 Rounds of 3 batches, scored by a tasting panel on 4 attributes (0–15). Rounds 1–2 were the team's starting set; Rounds 3–10 were chosen by EGBO, an earlier version of this engine (Feb–May 2026). Ingredient limits and the batch-total limit are inferred from the pilot's own batches.

Rebuild the two shipped projects from the CSVs: `python data/samples/yogurt_pilot/build.py` (writes the two `.json` files here). `tests/test_sample_projects.py` checks they match.
