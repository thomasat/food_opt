Food Optimizer helps food developers plan and iterate on formulations. Everything runs on your own computer; your formulations and results never leave your computer.

## Download and install

### Mac

**[Download FoodOptimizer-0.9.0.dmg](https://github.com/thomasat/food_opt/releases/download/v0.9.0/FoodOptimizer-0.9.0.dmg)** ({{dmg_mb}} MB)

1. Open the downloaded file. A window appears with the Food Optimizer icon, an Applications folder and a "Start Here" note.
2. Drag Food Optimizer onto the Applications folder, then open it from your Applications folder.
3. The first time, the app downloads about 1 GB of components and shows a progress bar. Setup time depends on your connection and computer. After that, no internet is needed.

Requires a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or newer. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.

**Updating from an earlier version?** Quit Food Optimizer first, then drag the new one onto Applications and choose Replace. Your projects are kept.

### Windows

**[Download FoodOptimizer-0.9.0-Windows.exe](https://github.com/thomasat/food_opt/releases/download/v0.9.0/FoodOptimizer-0.9.0-Windows.exe)** ({{exe_mb}} MB)

1. Open the downloaded file. Windows may not recognize the app yet. Check the publisher is Food System Innovations, then click More info, then Run anyway.
2. Click Install. No admin rights needed.
3. Open Food Optimizer from Start.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and 2 GB free. Works offline.

**Updating from an earlier version?** Close the app, run the new installer. Your projects are kept.

For IT teams: the IT sheet, the SBOM and the file list are under Assets.

Both downloads are listed under **Assets** at the bottom of this page. The two "Source code" entries there are for developers.
