Food Optimizer helps food developers plan and iterate on formulations. Everything runs on your own Mac; your formulations and results never leave your computer.

## Download and install

**[Download FoodOptimizer-0.8.0.dmg](https://github.com/thomasat/food_opt/releases/download/v0.8.0/FoodOptimizer-0.8.0.dmg)** (19 MB)

The same file is listed under **Assets** at the bottom of this page. It is the only file you need; the two "Source code" entries there are for developers.

1. Open the downloaded file. A window appears with the Food Optimizer icon, an Applications folder and a "Start Here" note.
2. Drag Food Optimizer onto the Applications folder, then open it from your Applications folder.
3. The first time, the app downloads about 1 GB of components and shows a progress bar. Setup time depends on your connection and computer. After that, no internet is needed.

Requires a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or newer. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings.

**Updating from an earlier version?** Quit Food Optimizer first, then drag the new one onto Applications and choose Replace. Your projects are kept.

## What is new in 0.8.0

- **Two examples to start from.** *Open the burger example* shows a project set up with calculations, pre-mixes, a process setting and limits, ready for you to generate a first Round. *Try the yogurt pilot* opens real tasting data from a plant-based yogurt-style pilot: 15 formulations scored and Round 6 made, with its results file ready to upload.
- **A clearer Review tab.** One headline with the best score so far; a *Best so far* chart of every formulation in the order made; your best formulation against each target; and the amounts in your 3 best formulations inside the ranges you allowed. No forecasts or verdicts: the measurements speak for themselves.
- **Results that show their working.** *How the score is worked out* shows each measurement, its goal, its share and the points it earned. Values above a target are orange, below are purple.
- **Make a round compares each suggestion with your best.** A table puts every suggestion next to your best formulation so far, colouring the amounts that moved.
- **No repeated formulations in the first Rounds.** In projects whose limits rule out part of the allowed amounts, a new Round, or *Discard this Round and generate again*, could hand back formulations you already had. It no longer does.
- **Error messages say where and what to do.** For example, "Butter: enter Lowest and Highest." instead of "Enter a number."; a damaged file, a full disk or a read-only folder now gives a plain message instead of a technical error.
- **A tidier Set up.** Ingredient properties have their own fold; the sidebar keeps *Open project* first and folds *New project* away while you work; open folds stay open.
- **Batch size.** The most a Round can make now respects your ingredient limits, and clearing the box returns each formulation to its own total.

Projects saved by earlier versions open unchanged.
