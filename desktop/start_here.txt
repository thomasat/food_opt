FOOD OPTIMIZER — START HERE

WHAT YOUR COMPUTER NEEDS
- A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later — any Mac from late 2020 onward).
  Not sure? Click the Apple menu (top left of your screen), choose
  "About This Mac", and look at the "Chip" line.
- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- About 6 GB of free disk space.
- An internet connection the FIRST time you open the app. Setup downloads
  about 1 GB of software components and usually takes a few minutes,
  up to 15 on a slow connection. After that, no internet is needed.

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Drag the Food Optimizer icon onto the Applications folder icon
   next to it.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Food Optimizer.
3. The first time, the app sets itself up: it downloads about 1 GB and
   usually takes a few minutes, up to 15 on a slow connection. The window
   shows a progress bar the whole way — leave it open. When it finishes,
   Food Optimizer opens on your screen, ready to use.
   If your Mac asks whether you are sure you want to open it, click Open.

TRYING IT OUT
- The app comes with a sample project: a plant-based burger with twenty
  ingredients and two panel scores. On the first screen, click
  "Try the sample project" to explore before setting up your own.
- Starting from a spreadsheet instead? The same screen offers a CSV
  template you can fill in and upload.

USING IT DAY TO DAY
- To open the app again later, open Food Optimizer from Applications,
  like any app. It opens in its own window.
- To quit, close the window — that's it.
- Your projects folder is one click away: File menu > Open Projects
  Folder. Need bigger text? View menu > Bigger Text.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Everything the app does happens on your Mac. Your recipes,
  experiments, and results never leave your computer.
- Your project files are saved in a folder called FoodOptimizer in your
  home folder (in Finder: click the Go menu, then Home). You can see,
  copy, or delete them at any time.
- The app's window talks only to your own Mac, never to a website.
  Nothing you do in it goes over the internet.
- The one-time setup downloads the app's software components. It sends
  nothing of yours anywhere — not then, not ever. No statistics,
  no tracking.

NEED HELP?
Please reach out to the Food Intelligence Lab at
https://github.com/thomasat/food_opt/issues. When you write in, describe
the problem in words. Do not attach project files, backups, or recipes,
because issues on that page are public. When you do, attaching
the app's technical details file helps us help you: inside the app,
the Help menu's "Show Log File" finds it for you. If the app will not
open, find it in Finder instead: click the Go menu, choose
"Go to Folder...", paste the line below, press Return, and attach
the file that opens:
  ~/Library/Application Support/FoodOptimizer/launcher.log
