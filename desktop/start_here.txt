FOOD OPTIMIZER — START HERE

WHAT YOUR COMPUTER NEEDS
- A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later — any Mac from late 2020 onward).
  Not sure? Click the Apple menu (top left of your screen), choose
  "About This Mac", and look at the "Chip" line.
- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- About 5 GB of free disk space.
- An internet connection the FIRST time you open the app (setup takes
  usually 1 to 5 minutes, depending on your internet speed). After that, no
  internet is needed.

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Drag the Food Optimizer icon onto the Applications folder icon
   next to it.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Food Optimizer.
3. The first time, the app sets itself up (usually 1 to 5 minutes). When it
   finishes, Food Optimizer opens on your screen, ready to use.

TRYING IT OUT
- Next to this file you'll find an "Example Data" folder with sample
  spreadsheets. Upload "example ingredients.csv" in the app's Ingredients
  section to see how everything works before using your own data.

USING IT DAY TO DAY
- To open the app again later, open Food Optimizer from Applications,
  like any app. It opens in its own window (or in your web browser).
- When you are done, just close the browser tab. The app shuts itself
  down on its own about 15 minutes later.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Everything the app does happens on your Mac. Your recipes,
  experiments, and results never leave your computer.
- Your project files are saved in a folder called FoodOptimizer in your
  home folder (in Finder: click the Go menu, then Home). You can see,
  copy, or delete them at any time.
- The app runs at an address called "localhost" — that means your own
  computer, not a website. Nothing you do in it goes over the internet.
- The one-time setup downloads the app's software components. It sends
  nothing of yours anywhere — not then, not ever. No statistics,
  no tracking.
