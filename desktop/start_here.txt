FOOD OPTIMIZER — START HERE

WHAT YOUR COMPUTER NEEDS
- A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later — any Mac from late 2020 onward).
  Not sure? Click the Apple menu (top left of your screen), choose
  "About This Mac", and look at the "Chip" line.
- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- About 6 GB of free disk space.
- An internet connection the first time you open the app. After that,
  no internet is needed.

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Drag the Food Optimizer icon onto the Applications folder icon
   next to it.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Food Optimizer.
3. The first time, the app sets itself up: it downloads about 1 GB of
   software components. On a good connection this takes under a minute;
   on a slow office network up to 15 minutes. The window shows a progress bar
   the whole way.
   Leave it open. When it finishes, Food Optimizer opens, ready to use.
   If your Mac asks whether you are sure you want to open it, click Open.

TRYING IT OUT
- The app comes with a sample project: a plant-based burger with eight
  ingredients and two panel scores. On the first screen, click
  "Try the sample project" to explore before setting up your own.
- Starting from a spreadsheet instead? The same screen offers a CSV
  template you can fill in and upload.

USING IT DAY TO DAY
- To open the app again, open Food Optimizer from Applications, like
  any app. It runs in its own window.
- To quit, close the window.
- Your projects folder is one click away: File menu › Open Projects
  Folder. Need bigger text? View menu › Bigger Text.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Your formulations and results never leave your Mac. The app
  does not connect to any website or service, and it collects no
  statistics.
- Your project files are saved in a folder called FoodOptimizer in your
  home folder (in Finder: click the Go menu, then Home). You can see,
  copy or delete them at any time.
- The one-time setup only downloads the app's software components. It
  sends nothing of yours anywhere.

NEED HELP?
Write to the Food Intelligence Lab at
https://github.com/thomasat/food_opt/issues. Describe the problem in
words, and do not attach project files, backups or formulations, because
that page is public.

Attaching the app's technical details file helps us help you. Inside
the app, choose Show Log File from the Help menu and it finds the file
for you. If the app will not open, find it in Finder instead: click the
Go menu, choose "Go to Folder...", paste the line below, press Return,
and attach the file that opens:
  ~/Library/Application Support/FoodOptimizer/launcher.log
