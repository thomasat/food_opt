FOOD OPTIMIZER — START HERE

INSTALL
1. Drag Food Optimizer into Applications.
2. Open Food Optimizer from Applications.
3. Keep the window open while the first-time setup downloads the required
   software (about 1 GB). Progress appears in the window.
   Setup time depends on your connection and computer.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 13 Ventura or newer,
about 6 GB of free space, and internet for first-time setup. Check your chip
in Apple menu → About This Mac. You can use the app offline after setup;
an app update may need another software download.

Updating? Quit the old app, then replace it in Applications. Your saved
projects are stored separately and are retained.

YOUR FIRST ROUND
1. Click “Try the sample project.” It is a 100 g burger teaching example
   with illustrative ingredients and targets, not a validated formulation.
2. On Set up, review the ingredients and measurements. Open a blend under
   Blend compositions to see its parts. Dry blend has fixed percentages;
   Fats and oils lets the component amounts vary separately.
3. Hydration water follows a rule based on the two proteins. Remaining
   water brings each formulation to 100 g. Their Lowest and Highest cells
   are empty because the rules determine the amounts. “Calculation help”
   explains this with examples.
4. Open “Preparation and records” to read the preparation method. Then
   click “Generate formulations” and choose how many to make.
5. Download the round sheets (Excel). The workbook has three tabs:
   • Round overview — the plan and shared preparation method.
   • Preparation — fixed-ratio pre-mixes to prepare for the round.
   • Results — measurements and any actual amounts you want to record.
   Tabs are at the bottom in Excel and at the top in Numbers. If a link
   does not open in Numbers, select the named tab directly.
6. Prepare each formulation separately and enter your measurements in
   the workbook's input cells. Return to Make a round, upload the workbook,
   review the imported values, and click Save results. Editing the
   workbook alone does not update the app. You can also type results
   directly into the app.
7. Review the results and start another round. The app uses your saved
   results to suggest what to try next.

SETTING UP YOUR OWN PROJECT
Create a project in the sidebar. Type into the ingredient and measurement
tables, then click Save changes. Use the same Lowest and Highest to keep
an amount or setting fixed. Use different values to let the app vary it.
“Import ingredients” provides a spreadsheet template and an upload option.

“Preparation and records” holds the batch size, steps shared by all
formulations, references, and optional records such as Lot and Actual
amounts. Choose “Add your own recording field” for other records, such as
Operator or Expiry date. Choose whether to record a value for each
formulation/trial or each ingredient in the round. Enter values in the
app or on the workbook’s Results tab. These records do not affect scores.
Use Measurements and targets
for outcomes you want scored. Ingredient limits are optional restrictions
such as a maximum fat content; the example already includes one.

For a fermentation study, add numeric process settings such as temperature
and time. A project containing only process settings uses trials and does
not need a batch size. Named categories such as strain are not varied by
this version. Different time points can be separate measurements, such as
pH at 6 h and pH at 24 h.

You can edit an unrecorded formulation in the round table before making it.
Use actual amounts/settings to record what you made if it differs from the
plan. The example enables Lot and Actual amounts; your own projects start
with optional records turned off.

YOUR FILES AND HELP
Projects are saved on your Mac in ~/FoodOptimizer. File → Open Projects
Folder opens that folder. Use “Save a copy of this project” before a major
change. “Clear project contents,” under Manage project, empties the project
while keeping its name and saving a copy first; it is not needed to start
a new round.

Open the app again from Applications. Close its window to quit.
View → Bigger Text increases the text size.
